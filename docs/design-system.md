# MMA Store visual system

Brief: Assignment 2's three screens and the existing gaming catalog. Skill: https://github.com/jiji262/claude-design-skill, installed in `~/.codex/skills/claude-design`.

Chosen direction: Player Store, with a forest-green typographic hero, white product photograph panel, ivory page surfaces and a responsive grid. Alternative compositions explored: compact bright storefront and editorial equipment catalog. The application uses Player Store consistently.

Colors: forest #102e27, primary #13745b, ivory #f7f8f2, white #ffffff, lime #d8ed9c. Be Vietnam Pro is self-hosted with its OFL license. Body 12–14 px; responsive display 28–54 px; spacing follows 4/8/16/24/32/48. Primary touch targets are at least 44 px. Product cards use TouchableOpacity feedback and actual catalog photographs, with no generated product imagery, fake reviews or invented sales numbers.

Product identity and descriptions are curated from the retailer catalog. Prices and availability come from its public Store API through the backend's CORS bridge. The composition is original to MMA Store.
